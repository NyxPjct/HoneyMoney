# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want HoneyMoney to be a welcoming community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. Many of our users are beginners: be patient and kind.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the people who use the app.
- Use inclusive language. Use someone's stated pronouns; when you don't know them, use neutral language.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Promoting scams, get-rich-quick schemes, gambling or trading signals in project spaces.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening a confidential report through GitHub's "Report abuse" feature or by contacting the maintainers listed in the repository. Maintainers will review every report, keep the reporter's identity private and may remove, edit or reject contributions, or ban participants, for behavior they deem harmful.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
